Welcome to JellySqueeze!

JellySqueeze is the essential video compression utility for your Jellyfin media server. Self-hosted video libraries pile up enormous files — a single episode can waste gigabytes of disk space on bitrates your TV can't even display!

Point JellySqueeze at your library and it recursively scans every video, re-encodes oversized files to a sensible target bitrate with GPU acceleration, and strips audio & subtitle tracks in languages you don't speak — all while keeping your existing H.264/HEVC files untouched. It remembers what it already squeezed, so you can stop and resume any time.

Features

  • GPU acceleration — NVIDIA NVENC, AMD VA-API, Intel QSV, or plain CPU
  • Target bitrate engine — only oversized files get re-encoded, the rest are skipped in milliseconds
  • Language cleanup — keep just the audio & subtitle languages you actually speak
  • Surround downmix — folds 7.1/5.1 audio down to stereo for non-surround setups
  • Resume-safe — every finished file is logged; stop anytime and pick up where you left off
  • Parallel workers — squeeze 2–4 files at once on capable hardware
  • Jellyfin "Recently Added" — nightly scans that only touch new arrivals
  • Scheduler — daily runs at 3 AM while you sleep
  • Atomic replacement — originals are swapped only after the new file passes validation
  • Live web UI — progress bars, logs and history over WebSocket
